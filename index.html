What's inside

A reading log that's fine with a short history

📚

Your whole library

Books you're reading, have finished, or just own — paperback, hardback, ebook, or audiobook. Narrow it to Reading, Finished, or Shelf, and each list sorts itself the way you'd expect.

📌

Want to Read

Keep the books people tell you about on their own list, with who recommended each one. Swipe to start reading when you're ready.

📷

Scan to add

Point the camera at the barcode and BookIt fills in the title and author. No barcode? Scan Cover reads them off the front.

✅

Start, finish, or set it aside

One tap to Start Reading, Mark Finished and rate it, or say I've read enough. Don't remember the dates from years ago? Leave them blank.

🔁

Re-reads

Read It Again keeps every read with its own dates and rating, and Log a Past Read backfills the one from years ago.

📄

Bring your Kindle & Audible library

Import a whole reading list from a spreadsheet — only the title is required — and export it back out to edit on your Mac.

How it works

Add what you're reading, then keep going

1

Add the book you're reading

Type it in or scan the barcode. Add a few you've finished, too — dates are optional.

2

Save the recommendations

Put each book someone mentions on Want to Read, with their name, so you don't forget.

3

Finish, rate, and look back

Mark it finished and give it stars. The Stats tab shows how many you've read this year and how fast.

Your reading log, always with you

BookIt keeps your books safe in iCloud. No account to create — just the Apple ID you already use. And a one-file backup is a tap away on the About tab.
