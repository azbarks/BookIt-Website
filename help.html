🚀 Getting Started

BookIt is a reading log. It keeps track of the books you've read, the one you're reading now, the audiobooks you've listened to, and the ones you want to get to — with dates, a star rating, and who recommended each one. It's built to look fine even if you're just starting out and only have a handful of books.

Your first few minutes in the app

  1. On the Library tab, tap + and add the book you're reading now. It starts as Reading, with today as the start date.
  2. Add a few books you've already finished. Set the Status to Finished and give it a rating — if you don't remember the dates, just leave the date switches off.
  3. On the Want to Read tab, tap + for each book someone's told you about, and put their name in Recommended by.
  4. When you finish the current book, open it and tap Mark Finished, then tap the stars.
Have a big Kindle or Audible library? You don't have to type it in. See Spreadsheet import.

🔖 The five statuses

Every book has a status — where you are with reading it:

On the Shelf Want to Read Reading Finished Gave Up
  • On the Shelf — you have it, but no plans to read it right now. Most of a purchased library lands here.
  • Want to Read — next up, or someone recommended it. These live on their own tab.
  • Reading — in progress.
  • Finished — done. You can rate it 1 to 5 stars.
  • Gave Up — you read enough and set it aside. No guilt; it's just a note for later.

The buttons on a book's page move it along — see A book's page.

📚 Library

Every book except your Want to Read list. Each row shows the format (🎧 audiobook, ebook, paperback, hardback), the title and author, the status, the star rating, and when you started or finished it.

All / Reading / Finished / Shelf

The bar at the top narrows the list. Each choice sorts itself the way you'd expect — the line underneath shows how many books there are and how they're sorted:

ChoiceShowsSorted by
AllEverything, including books you gave up onNewest added first
ReadingBooks in progressNewest started first
FinishedBooks you've finishedNewest finished first (undated ones at the bottom)
ShelfOn the Shelf booksTitle, A to Z (ignoring "The", "A", "An")

Search

Pull down to search by title, author, notes, or source.

Format and archived books

The filter button at the top left shows just one format — only audiobooks, say — and has a Show Archived switch. See Archive.

Little tags on a row

  • ↻ ×2 — you've read it more than once. See Re-reads.
  • Subscription / Borrowed / Sample — you don't own this copy. See Ownership.

Swipe left on a row to delete a book.

📌 Want to Read

Your wishlist, newest first. Each row shows who recommended the book and the start of your notes. The number on the tab tells you how many are waiting.

  • Swipe right on a book and tap Start to begin reading it — it moves to the Library as Reading, with today as the start date.
  • Swipe left to delete it.
  • Search by title, author, or who recommended it — handy for "what was that book Dave told me about?"

📊 Stats

A quick look at your reading — nothing to set up, no goals to hit. It's all worked out from your books:

  • Overview — total books, and how many are Reading, Finished, Set aside, Want to Read, On the Shelf, and Archived.
  • This year — books finished this year, your average rating, and your typical time to read a book.
  • Pace — your quickest and longest reads (needs at least two books with both dates).
  • Finished by Year — how many you finished each year.
  • By Format — how your books split between audiobook, ebook, paperback and hardback.

➕ Adding a book

Tap + on the Library tab (the book starts as Reading) or the Want to Read tab (it starts as Want to Read). Only the title is required.

FieldWhat it's for
Title, AuthorThe title is the only thing you must fill in.
FormatAudiobook, Ebook, Paperback, or Hardback.
StatusSee the five statuses.
OwnershipOwn it, Subscription, Borrowed, or Sample — see Ownership.
DatesStart and finish dates, each with an "I have a date" switch — see Unknown dates.
Rating1 to 5 stars. Appears once the status is Finished.
Recommended byWho told you about it.
SourceWhere your copy is from — Kindle, an Audible account, a library.
Why I set it asideAppears when the status is Gave Up. Optional.
NotesAnything you want to remember about it.

To change a book later, open it and tap Edit. Cancel throws away your changes.

📷 Scan a barcode or cover

Holding a paper book? Let the camera fill in the title and author. When you're adding a new book, the top of the form has two buttons:

Scan Barcode

Point the camera at the barcode on the back cover (the one starting 978 or 979). BookIt looks the book up online and fills in the title and author. The format is set to Paperback — change it if it's a hardback.

Scan Cover

For a book with no barcode, or one the lookup can't find. Take a picture of the front cover; BookIt reads the text on it right on your phone and takes its best guess — the biggest words as the title, a name as the author.

If it guesses wrong, every line it read is listed under Text on the cover. Tap the right line for the title, then tap the right line for the author.

Scanning is optional. You can always just type the title in. The scan buttons don't appear when you're editing a book you already have.

📖 A book's page

Tap any book to see everything about it. Near the top are buttons for the next step, which change with the book's status:

When it's…You can…
On the ShelfMove to Want to Read, or Start Reading
Want to ReadStart Reading
ReadingMark Finished, or I've read enough (moves it to Gave Up)
FinishedRead It Again, or Undo — Not Finished Yet
Gave UpMove back to Reading
  • Start Reading fills in today as the start date, unless one is already set.
  • Mark Finished fills in today as the finish date and shows the rating stars, so you can rate it right there.
  • I've read enough asks to make sure, then moves the book to Gave Up and takes it off your Reading list. Tap Edit afterward if you want to note why.
  • Undo — Not Finished Yet is for when you tapped Mark Finished by mistake.

Below the buttons: the dates and how long it took you, ownership, rating, reading history, who recommended it, and your notes. At the bottom are Archive and Delete Book.

📅 Unknown dates

You won't remember when you read most older books, and that's fine. In the add/edit screen, the start and finish dates each have a switch — I have a start date and I have a finish date. Leave a switch off and BookIt stores no date, rather than a made-up one. The book shows "—" for that date.

When both dates are known, BookIt shows how long the book took you ("9 days", "3 weeks", "2 months"), and it counts toward your pace stats.

🔁 Re-reads

Some books are worth reading more than once. BookIt keeps every read.

Read It Again

On a Finished book, tap Read It Again. Your last read — its dates and rating — is saved to the book's Reading History, and the book starts over as Reading with today as the start date.

Log a Past Read

Read it years ago before you started using BookIt? In the Reading History section, tap Log a Past Read and add it — dates and a rating if you have them, or just a note. Even "I read it once in college" is worth logging.

Seeing your reads

The Reading History section lists every read, with the current one marked. Its heading says "Read 3 Times" once there's more than one, and the book's row in the Library gets a ↻ ×3 tag. Swipe left on a past read to delete one you added by mistake.

🏷 Ownership & source

Status is where you are with reading a book. Ownership is whether you still have it. They're separate — you can finish a library book, or own one you'll never read.

  • Own it — the default.
  • Subscription — you read it through Kindle Unlimited, an Audible plan, or similar, and no longer have it.
  • Borrowed — from a friend or the library.
  • Sample — just the free sample.

Change it right on the book's page with the I have this as picker. Anything other than Own it shows as a small tag on the Library row.

Source is free text for where your copy came from — "Kindle", "Audible-main", a second Audible account, "christianaudio", the library. It's searchable from the Library tab.

🗄 Archive

Archiving tucks a book out of the way without deleting it — a sample you'll never finish, a duplicate copy, a book you gave away. Tap Archive at the bottom of the book's page.

Archived books are hidden from the Library list. To see them, tap the filter button at the top left of the Library and turn on Show Archived — the list switches to only archived books and the title changes to "Archived". Open one and tap Unarchive to bring it back.

📄 Spreadsheet import & export

Typing in hundreds of books one by one is no fun. If you have a list — from Amazon's "Request My Data" for Kindle and Audible, or one you've kept yourself — save it as a CSV file and import it all at once.

Import Reading List

Go to About → Import Reading List (CSV) and pick the file. The first row must be the column names. Only a Title column is required; everything else is optional:

ColumnWhat goes in it
TitleRequired.
AuthorFree text.
FormatAudiobook, Ebook, Paperback, Hardback. "Kindle" and "Audible" work too. Blank → Ebook.
StatusOn the Shelf, Want to Read, Reading, Finished, Gave Up. "Read", "DNF" and "Unread" work too. Blank → On the Shelf.
OwnershipOwn it, Subscription, Borrowed, Sample. "KU" and "Audible" work too. Blank → Own it.
Archived"yes" to hide it from the Library list.
Start, FinishA full date (4/2/2026 or 2026-04-02), a month and year ("July 2019"), or just a year ("2019").
Rating0 to 5.
RecommendedByWho told you about it.
SourceWhere it's from.
GaveUpNoteWhy you set it aside.
NotesAnything else.

Column names don't care about capital letters or spaces. The same list is in the app — tap About → Column Guide.

When the import finishes, it tells you how many books it added, how many it skipped because you already have them, and how many dates it couldn't read (those are left blank).

Safe to repeat. A row whose title (and author) is already in your library is skipped, so importing the same file twice never makes duplicates.

Export Reading List

About → Export Reading List (CSV) saves every book to a spreadsheet you can open in Numbers or Excel, using the same columns the import reads. The file is named like 2026-10-04_1830_BookIt_Books.csv.

Editing your books on a Mac

To fix a lot of books at once: export, make your changes in Numbers or Excel, then Delete All Books and import the edited file. (You have to delete first — otherwise the import skips every book because it's already there.) Export a backup before you start, just in case.

Re-reads don't go in the spreadsheet. It has one row per book — only the current read. If you've used Read It Again or Log a Past Read, the JSON backup is the only file that keeps that history.

💾 Backup & Restore

With iCloud on, your books are already synced and safe in your iCloud account. A backup file is an extra copy you control.

Export Backup

About → Export Backup saves one file with every book — including all your re-reads — named like 2026-10-04_1830_BookIt_Backup.json. Save it to Files, Dropbox, or email it to yourself.

Import Backup

About → Import Backup and pick a backup file. Import merges with what you already have: books already in the app are updated from the file, missing ones are added, and nothing is ever deleted. Importing the same file twice is safe. Backups from older versions of BookIt still restore.

A good habit: export a backup every month or two, and always before Delete All Books.

❓ FAQ

Where did my Want to Read books go in the Library?

They're on their own tab, Want to Read, so the Library stays about books you have or have read. Once you start one, it moves to the Library.

I can't find a book I gave up on.

Gave Up books only show under All in the Library — not Reading, Finished, or Shelf. Or just search for it.

A book disappeared from my Library.

It's probably archived. Tap the filter button at the top left, turn on Show Archived, and look there. Also check the Format filter isn't set to a single format.

Why can't I rate a book I'm reading?

The stars appear once a book is Finished. Tap Mark Finished and they show up right on the book's page.

I don't see Scan Barcode or Scan Cover.

They only appear when you're adding a new book. If BookIt asked to use the camera and you said no, turn it back on in Settings → BookIt → Camera on your iPhone.

The barcode scan said "No match".

Not every book is in the online book databases — older and smaller-press books especially. Try Scan Cover, or type the title in.

My spreadsheet import skipped books I changed.

The import skips any title you already have, so it can't make duplicates — but that also means it won't update them. To apply edits, export a backup, then About → Delete All Books, then import your edited spreadsheet.

Some of my dates didn't come in.

BookIt only fills in a date it's sure of. Something vaguer than a year ("a while ago", "2010s") is left blank, and the import tells you how many it skipped. Fix those in the spreadsheet, or open the books and add the dates.

Does BookIt work without iCloud?

Yes. Everything is stored on your device whether or not iCloud is on — you just won't get sync to your other devices. If you're not using iCloud, export a backup now and then so you have a copy.

Is there an iPad version?

No — BookIt is made for iPhone. With iCloud on, your books are kept safe in your iCloud account and come along when you move to a new iPhone.

How do I report a bug or ask for a feature?

Head to the Support page and send an email. Include your build number (About tab) and what you expected versus what happened.
